    Roadmap to Platinum — tap to expand

    Built from the official descriptions: chapters and named missions first, collectible counts and activity totals as cleanup. Trophies flagged MISSABLE are tied to a single named mission, a character strand, or a character challenge in their official description — clear them before the finale.

    1. Step 1 — Story spine. Play Gone Fishing, then Chapters 1–4 in order (Just Like Paradise → Paradise Lost → T**s Ass Drug Money → Vice City, Baby! → Top Of The World, Ma). These cannot be missed, but pause at each chapter break for Step 2.
    2. Step 2 — Named missions & strands as they appear. Whenever a named mission (Breakfast Run, The Cartel Heist, Child's Play, Headliners, VCIA Takedown, Operation Savage Glory) or a character strand (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone) opens, finish it before advancing the story. Eight completed strands also earn Strangers & Freaks. Character tasks for Cal (fishing) and Bret (the hunt) belong in this step too.
    3. Step 3 — Finale gate. Before the final mission (You Gotta Know When to Get Off), open the Missable filter and confirm every flagged trophy is ticked. Treat the finale as a point of no return for strand content.
    4. Step 4 — Collectibles cleanup. Explore all 52 areas of Leonida, meditate at all 18 spots, complete 3 Collectible Card sets, WAiNK It at 100 locations, discover 100 vehicles, buy all 8 garage properties, and register 10 vehicles in the Scooter Bros app.
    5. Step 5 — Activities cleanup. Gold all 42 Shooting Range challenges, win Top Fun drone races at all 14 locations, win 20 different races, complete 250 stunts and 50 challenges, try all 9 activity types, rob 5 bank vaults, one illegal operation per gang (9), and finish the one-off feats (Sneakthief, Gunslinger, Hot Together, Retail Therapy…).
    6. Step 6 — Money & 100%. Launder $250,000 of Marked Bills, spend $100,000 across shops, and mop up the cryptic one-offs (Challenging Tank, Personal Best, The Truth Is Out There) using their guides. Attain 100% Completion (A Long Road), and the platinum — Vice Got a Price! — unlocks itself.
